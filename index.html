Keeps

Your Claude Code sessions, on your phone. A menu-bar app for macOS.

Download for Mac

Apple silicon and Intel · requires macOS 14 or later

First launch

Keeps is signed but not notarized by Apple, so macOS refuses the first launch. This is the expected path, not a broken download:

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Keeps to Applications.
  2. Open it once. macOS will refuse.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
